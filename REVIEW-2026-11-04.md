## Review decisions and remaining launch work

Apple's branding HIG supports custom headline fonts with system/body fonts.
One font family and a 300 KB whole-page budget are not stated Apple requirements.
The existing font pairing stays; the heavy assets and small labels were fixed.
See https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/branding and
https://developer.apple.com/app-store/marketing/guidelines/ for the actual guidance.

Brevo onboarding/business address, sender authentication, a receiving support
mailbox, end-to-end signup, final legal review, current app captures and app-claim
verification remain open. No emails sent, no new data collection, no production
release, and no Apple approval/accessibility-conformance claim is implied.
