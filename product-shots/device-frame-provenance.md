# Official iPhone 18 Pro Max Glacier artwork

The November 6, 2026 preview uses Apple's original portrait PNG in both hero phones,
all three nutrition/strength/progress product stages, and the app-screen showcase. The same
artwork also appears in the social-share composition. The enlarged-screen
dialog intentionally shows the original capture without hardware so readers
can inspect the app content.

The project owner confirmed separate Apple permission for this use. Using the
package accepts its Design Resources license. This is app marketing artwork,
not a reusable asset pack, template download, or endorsement by Apple. Future
reuse must remain within the applicable permission and Apple terms.

The PNG in `dist/assets/brand/iphone-18-pro-max-glacier-apple.png` is byte-for-byte
identical to Apple's supplied file. It is displayed upright and whole, without
recoloring, added shadows, filters, reflections or phone animation. Decorative
overlays were removed from the app screen. Apple trademark attribution is in
the footer and the social-share composition.

## App captures and geometry

The screen opening is 1320 × 2868, offset 75 pixels from the left and 66 from the
top. The screenshot is placed beneath the original artwork with proportional
containment. Native app content and original capture files remain unchanged.
See `provenance.json` for capture history. These are existing iPhone 17 Pro
simulator captures, not a new iPhone 18 Pro Max compatibility test. Their original
status bars and the older nutrition capture's in-app logo remain unchanged;
updated native captures remain a launch-review task.

The previous Hoverify illustration is no longer referenced in the public page.
Glacier is the official finish name for the requested light-blue appearance.
